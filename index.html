How to read the Amazon FBA columns

On hand
Physically in an Amazon bin and sellable today.
Reserved
At an FC but committed — customer orders being picked, FC-to-FC transfers, and MCF website orders. Not available to sell.
Researching
Amazon cannot account for the units and is investigating internally. Treat as zero for planning.
Unfulfillable
At an FC but not sellable — damaged or defective. Needs a removal order; treat as zero for planning.
Inbound — in transit
On an open shipment that is genuinely moving. Will become on-hand.
Inbound — unlocated
Still carried as inbound on a shipment Amazon has finished receiving. Never scanned; recoverable only through an investigation. Zero for planning.
FBA total
Amazon's own sum of every column above. It overstates what is sellable.
